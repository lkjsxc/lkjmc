# E-QUALITY technique disposition 2026-07-11

## Purpose

Interpret the bounded E-QUALITY evidence without adopting a product design or
altering controller state.

## Disposition

`combine` for future experiment work only. Retain the property/fuzz,
state/fault, real loopback load/fault, checker-mutation, secret-canary, and
Compose migration/restore harnesses as separate research evidence. Do not merge
any product behavior, select an architecture, or add a state-matrix claim from
this result.

## Evidence and comparison

The baseline and result commits, exact commands, repetitions, faults, raw
locations, failures, and blocked protocol attempt are in
[E-QUALITY run](../runs/e-quality-20260711.md). The combined state/fault test
proved duplicate durability across a test-only pre-effect fault; the combined
load/fault run proved transport loss under an actual isolated daemon kill and a
fresh-process doctor recovery. Compose proved database migration cases and one
full-database restore followed by daemon boot.

No product defect was found by the bounded property, parser, seed, checker, or
canary cases. Three experiment-harness defects were found and fixed: a temporary
parser corpus lifetime, a missing Compose environment export, and load workers
that could spin on an EOF after a daemon kill. The repair bounds requests and
joins, cancels stragglers, cleans process groups, and asserts no survivors in
its raw artifacts. Neither defect is product evidence. The checker mutation
yielded deliberate rejection evidence; the guarded protocol command remained
blocked.

## Constraints and costs

Correctness and secret invariants held within their stated boundaries. The
property and state evidence is deterministic but narrow; test-only failpoints
are not release effects. The local debug doctor timing has no database write,
JVM plugin, Minecraft, resource, or capacity meaning. Its timeout-based budget
is retained only to bound the harness, not as an SLO. The first Compose
all-mode command failed and the corrected second restore-only command passed;
that first-failure/second-success harness correction cannot establish a flake
rate or erase the failure. Protocol evidence remains absent because the
withdrawn Java daemon adapter has no bounded local-safe client.

The repaired maintenance cost remains test-only with no new dependency.
Compose is materially slower and requires Docker; future users should choose it
for migration or restore risk rather than routine parser coverage.

whether any technique supports a later adoption; no adoption or cleanup task is
created here. Preserve commits `a7f5a21`, `c1e2555`, and `3923f56` and the ignored artifact
roots for reproduction.
