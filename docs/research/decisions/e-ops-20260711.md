# E-OPS research decision 2026-07-11

## Purpose

Dispose of the operations experiment without adopting a runtime, release, or
external-service claim.

## Disposition

`reject` the combined candidate for adoption now. Keep the failed clean-Compose
and passing restore observations as research evidence only. The local
immutable-toolchain finding and the Compose lock-timeout failure reject the
candidate; rootless Docker, Kubernetes, and signing remain external proof
pending rather than passed.

## Evidence and invariants

The evidence is the [hypothesis](../experiments/e-ops-20260711.md) and the
[E-OPS run](../runs/e-ops-20260711.md). The runner at `4a1a018` retained
redacted ignored output with index SHA-256
`b4e24272a0265585a75540c52e8ddd22e4cc79e529a1884c96d71754963bc82e`.
Its credential-canary scan and post-removal unique-image absence probe passed.
It did not alter product source, owner contracts, controller state, or state
matrices.

The clean Compose gate failed at a PostgreSQL lock timeout. PostgreSQL
restore/daemon `doctor`, two matching release-daemon hashes, test-only fault
boundary inspection, and a constrained user systemd scope passed. They do not
prove player traffic, a support bundle, a production systemd unit, rootless
execution, cluster behavior, signing, or a clean verification gate.

## Reason and combinations

A source tag is not immutable acquisition evidence. The Dockerfile lacks a
pinned base-image digest and Rustup checksum; the Gradle wrapper lacks its
distribution checksum. Adopting a release or supply-chain statement on these
facts would be false.

The Compose and restore candidates can combine with a later immutable-toolchain
change, independently reviewed rootless engine, and a dedicated support-bundle
and loaded fault scenario. The systemd observation is useful only as a narrow
sandbox candidate; it must not replace a service-unit design. Kubernetes needs
a client, authorized disposable namespace, config, database URL, and cluster
credentials. Signing needs a trusted key and a signed source or artifact.

## Reconsideration and follow-up

Reconsider only after all acquisition checks are immutable, a fresh clean
Compose run succeeds without the lock timeout, and each external lane records
its guarded command and observed effect. A fault/support candidate also needs
a real bounded workload, redacted generated support artifact, and negative
secret scan.
