## Evidence and disposition

The catalog is source evidence for the 150 imported IDs. E-CONTRACT, E-CONTROL,
E-DATA, E-JVM, E-OBS, E-OPS, E-PRODUCT, E-QUALITY, E-RUNTIME, and E-SECURITY
have bounded research records in the sibling indexes; their decisions do not
adopt product behavior. Other IDs have no run or decision. Record hypotheses,
reproducible runs, and adoption or rejection before changing product docs.

## Decision boundary

Only an owner contract and state evidence can describe shipped behavior. A
research ID may become an implementation task only after its decision cites a
real run and preserves reconsideration conditions.
