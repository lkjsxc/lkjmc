# Product research ideas

## Purpose

Imported experience candidates; none changes player or operator behavior today.

## Catalog evidence

Source: supplied `experiments/catalog/product.md`. Each remains untested.

## Candidates

- `PX-OPERATOR-JOURNEY` end-to-end operations; `PX-PLAYER-ONBOARD` first join.
- `PX-TASK-MENU` goal navigation; `PX-WAKE-PROGRESS` wake-state visibility.
- `PX-TRANSFER-RECOVERY` safe recovery; `PX-CLAIM-VISUAL` claim context.
- `PX-PROGRESSION` exploit-resistant loop; `PX-ADVENTURE-CATALOG` verified assets.
- `PX-SOCIAL-ENTRY` discoverable social journey; `PX-ADMIN-CONSOLE` truthful views.
- `PX-CHAT-FALLBACK` critical non-menu actions; `PX-PLAYER-DOCS` help corpus choice.
- `PX-BEDROCK-UX` supported Bedrock limits; `PX-ACCESSIBILITY` non-color interaction.
- `PX-LOCALE-TONE` Japanese/English wording; `PX-FEATURE-PRUNE` value-based removal.
- `PX-DEGRADED-NETWORK` coherent outages.

## Decision boundary

Test journeys in combinations, including progression exploits and degraded paths.
Live player/client evidence needs the relevant consent, endpoint, and test
accounts; absent access is external proof pending, not a product result.
